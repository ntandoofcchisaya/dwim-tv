Add a Video

Paste any YouTube link or 11-character video ID — sermons, services, worship or prayers. The real title & channel are fetched from YouTube automatically.

🔴 Live Stream Control

Paste your YouTube live stream URL before a Sunday Service. The site shows an ON AIR banner and the "Watch Live" button joins the stream. Clear it after the service ends.

Catalog

Click Edit to change a video's title, channel, description, tags or thumbnail. Click the pin to feature a video at the top of its channel. Drag rows by the ☰ handle to reorder.

Edit Video

Paste a direct image link (.jpg/.png/.webp) to override the auto-fetched YouTube thumbnail — e.g. a custom poster or cropped photo hosted anywhere. Clear the field to go back to the YouTube thumbnail.

⚙ Channels

Rename, re-icon, delete or reorder channels. Drag rows by the ☰ handle to change the order shown on the site. Deleting a channel moves its videos to Ministry Archive (or another channel you choose) — nothing is ever deleted along with it.
